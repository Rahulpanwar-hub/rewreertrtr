Sign of Work:
• $0^\circ \le \theta < 90^\circ$: Positive Work (speeds object up)
• $\theta = 90^\circ$: Zero Work (e.g., centripetal force)
• $90^\circ < \theta \le 180^\circ$: Negative Work (opposes motion, e.g., friction)
